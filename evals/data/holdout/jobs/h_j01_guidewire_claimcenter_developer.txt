Guidewire ClaimCenter Developer (Configuration/Integration)
Sample Staffing Partners | Client: Example Casualty Group
Location: Hartford, CT (Hybrid, 3 days onsite)
Duration: 12 months, contract to hire
Rate: $70 - $78/hr on W2

Job Description:
Our client, a regional property and casualty carrier, is moving its claims platform from ClaimCenter 9 on premises to Guidewire Cloud. We are looking for a hands-on ClaimCenter developer to join the claims IT team.

Responsibilities:
- Configure ClaimCenter PCF screens, typelists, entity extensions and business rules for FNOL, exposures, reserves and payments
- Build and maintain inbound and outbound integrations using Guidewire Integration Gateway, REST APIs and messaging
- Support upgrade and cloud migration activities, including code merge and defect fixing
- Write GUnit tests and participate in code reviews
- Work with business analysts and QA in two-week sprints

Must Have:
1. 7+ years of overall IT experience
2. 4+ years of Guidewire ClaimCenter configuration experience
3. Strong Gosu programming skills
4. Experience with ClaimCenter 10 or Guidewire Cloud Platform
5. Experience with SOAP or REST web service integrations
6. Working knowledge of SQL on Oracle or SQL Server
7. Excellent verbal and written communication skills

Nice to Have:
- Guidewire ClaimCenter Configuration certification
- Experience with Guidewire Integration Gateway or Apache Camel
- Exposure to Guidewire Jutro digital platform
- Experience with Apache Kafka
- Experience with TeamCity or Jenkins build pipelines
- Knowledge of ISO ClaimSearch or other claims industry integrations

Only W2 candidates. US citizens, green card holders and candidates on H1B transfer are welcome to apply. No C2C or third-party agencies please.
