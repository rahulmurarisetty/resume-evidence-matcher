Site Reliability Engineer

Placeholder Cloud Services is hiring a Site Reliability Engineer to keep our payments platform fast and available. The role is remote within the United States.

Key Responsibilities:
* Operate and scale Kubernetes clusters running more than 150 services
* Define SLOs, manage error budgets and lead incident response
* Build and maintain infrastructure as code
* Improve CI/CD pipelines and release safety
* Participate in an on-call rotation (one week in six)

Qualifications:
* 5+ years of experience in software engineering, DevOps or systems administration
* Production experience with Kubernetes
* Strong experience with Terraform or Pulumi
* Experience with AWS or GCP
* Experience with Prometheus, Grafana or Datadog
* Scripting in Python, Go or Bash
* CI/CD experience with GitHub Actions or Jenkins
* Calm and clear communicator during incidents

Preferred:
* Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) or similar certification
* Experience with service mesh tools such as Istio or Linkerd
* Experience in payments or financial services
* Experience with Argo CD or Flux

Compensation: $160,000 - $190,000 base, plus equity.
Placeholder Cloud Services provides equal employment opportunity to all applicants.
