Senior Data Engineer
Example Analytics Co. | Remote (US)

About Us
Example Analytics Co. builds data products for property and casualty insurers. We are a team of 120 people, and our platform processes more than two billion policy and claims records a year.

Responsibilities
- Design, build and maintain batch and streaming data pipelines on Databricks
- Model claims, policy and billing data in Snowflake for analytics and actuarial teams
- Orchestrate and monitor workflows with Airflow
- Work with analysts and data scientists to define data contracts and quality checks
- Mentor junior engineers and review their code

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software or data engineering experience
- Strong Python and SQL skills
- Hands-on experience with PySpark and Databricks
- Experience with Snowflake or Redshift
- Experience building data pipelines on AWS or Azure
- Experience with dbt
- Familiarity with Git and CI/CD workflows

Nice to have
- Experience with Kafka or Kinesis
- Infrastructure as code with Terraform
- Experience with GitHub Actions
- Background in the insurance industry

What we offer
- Salary range: $150,000 - $175,000 plus annual bonus
- Medical, dental and vision insurance
- 401(k) with 5% match
- Four weeks of paid time off
