Job Title: .NET Full Stack Developer

Must have

- Strong C# and ASP.NET Core skills
- Experience with Angular 12 or later
- SQL Server and Entity Framework experience
- Experience deploying to Azure (App Service, Functions or AKS)
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills

Good to have

- Docker or Kubernetes
- Knowledge of Guidewire or other insurance platforms
- Experience with RxJS and NgRx
