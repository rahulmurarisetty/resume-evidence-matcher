Senior Data Engineer

About Northwind Analytics
Northwind Analytics helps regional insurers turn claims and policy data into faster decisions. We are a remote-first team of 40.

What you'll do
- Design and build batch and streaming data pipelines on Databricks and Snowflake
- Own data models for claims, policy and billing data used by actuarial and finance teams
- Orchestrate workflows with Airflow and keep them observable
- Partner with analysts to define metrics and data contracts
- Mentor two junior engineers and review their code

Requirements
- 5+ years of experience in data engineering
- Strong Python and SQL
- Hands-on experience with PySpark on Databricks
- Experience with Snowflake or another cloud data warehouse
- Experience building ETL pipelines on AWS
- Comfortable with Git and CI/CD practices

Nice to have
- Experience with dbt
- Kafka or other streaming tools
- Familiarity with Kubernetes
- Insurance industry experience is a plus

Benefits
- Medical, dental and vision coverage
- 401(k) with 4% match
- Home office stipend

Northwind Analytics is an equal opportunity employer.
